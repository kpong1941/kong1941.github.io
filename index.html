About Me

I'm an IT professional with 15+ years of experience across telecommunications, fintech, and financial services, now making a deliberate transition into dedicated cybersecurity roles.

My background spans Tier 1–3 NOC engineering at AT&T, IT operations at the BitMEX crypto exchange, and most recently a Technical Lead Security Specialist role at Westpac — where I worked on CIS compliance, SOX/GS007 audits, CVE lifecycle management, and security device configuration across a large enterprise environment. While I've worked alongside tools like CrowdStrike, Splunk, Elastic, Qualys, and Zscaler in a monitoring and visibility capacity, I'm now actively building my hands-on detection and response skills.

To bridge that gap, I've been working on practical projects: an Azure SOC lab simulating real-world attacks — password spray, privilege escalation, and NSG misconfiguration — detected using Microsoft Sentinel and KQL; a Python-based Security Log Analyzer for firewall anomaly detection; and a collection of incident response runbooks covering ransomware, DDoS, insider threats, and critical vulnerabilities.

I hold an ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) certification and am currently working towards my CISSP. I'm looking to bring my strong operational foundation and cross-functional experience into a role where I can grow as a threat analyst and make a meaningful contribution to a security team.
